Hello, my name is Javier Solorzano and I am a software engineer based in Baton Rouge, LA.

While working at Apple, I led development on products and features that shipped across iOS, macOS, visionOS, and tvOS. Working alongside the designers there taught me how to approach product design and build delightful software. I also hold a BS in Computer Engineering from LSU and an MS in Computer Science from GA Tech specializing in Human-Computer Interaction.

I’m currently working on my own projects and taking on freelance work. This blog is a place where I publish my thoughts on software engineering and product design.

My hobbies include baking sourdough, playing video games, and reading. I am a devout Catholic, husband to an amazing wife, and father to two beautiful children.
